# OpenV Group PDF-to-Homepage Integration Brief

## Content Strategy

The PDF’s strongest homepage story is not the full service catalogue. It is the **consolidation advantage**: OpenV Group replaces fragmented suppliers with one accountable relationship across managed IT, digital growth, and connected business software. The homepage should communicate that promise visually, prove it with a small number of metrics, then let the three brands and operating-layer section explain how it works.

| PDF theme | Homepage treatment | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| One group replacing multiple disconnected vendors | New compact **Group Advantage** section | Add one visually led section with a short headline, four proof metrics, and three outcome statements |
| 20+ years, 45 ICT professionals, 8 corporate partners, 3 integrated brands | Proof strip inside Group Advantage | Use only these four figures; avoid a separate stats-heavy section |
| One call, one invoice, total accountability | Group Advantage headline and outcomes | Prioritise accountability, simplicity, and predictable technology spend |
| Managed IT, digital capability, and business software | Hero support copy and operating-layer copy | Refine existing copy rather than adding another explanatory section |
| Detailed brand capabilities | Existing three brand cards | Replace generic descriptions and tags with concise PDF-informed positioning |
| Practical AI across Microsoft, security, ShiftBridge, and marketing | Existing capability lanes | Integrate into capability copy and tags; do not add a separate AI section |
| Active partner relationships, training, support, and preferential pricing | Existing Technology Partners intro | Strengthen the partner section’s supporting sentence without adding more partner cards |
| South African group with Gqeberha and Cape Town presence | Hero/footer copy | Add concise local context in supporting copy and footer |

## Approved Homepage Flow

The expanded flow remains visual and concise: **Hero and brands → Fragmented Vendors to One Group → Group Advantage → One Operating Layer → Applied AI → Technology Partners → Footer**. The two additional visual stories communicate the PDF’s core problem and its practical AI approach without reproducing long service catalogues.

## Content Exclusions

The homepage should not reproduce the PDF’s detailed product catalogues, vendor market statistics, long partner profiles, or full AI explainer. Those belong on future solution, brand, or partner detail pages. This prevents the landing page from becoming text-heavy and keeps the premium enterprise hierarchy intact.
